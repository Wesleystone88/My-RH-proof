# Independent check of `RHX.h8_frame`

**DERIVED — Verdict: different as statements; same pointwise inequality.** For a translate represented in the family at a sufficiently large scale, `FrameF` supplies every clause of Rubinstein’s H8. It also supplies common error bounds and a cap on the error. The paper states H8 for one fixed scale and translate. The Lean wrapper is a conditional family theorem with `0 < CH`. These distinctions matter when citing what has been proved.
