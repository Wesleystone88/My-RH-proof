## H4 — same three numerical bounds

**READ.** Rubinstein quantifies (III.326) over “all words $w,w'$”. In that inequality, $\log \mathrm{Lv}(w)$ lies between $\sum_{j=1}^m(\langle\log w_{u,j}\rangle+\log(\mathrm{rd}_E/(2π)))+m\ell_g-c_4(L_a+m^2L_a/k)$ and $c_4 m x^ε$. **DERIVED.** `RHX.h4_word_counts` gives all three with one nonnegative C at every sufficiently late index. The theorem `card_Tau` proves this is the reciprocal of the number of places, and `lgG` is the logarithm of the product of the two auxiliary level factors $g_0,g_1$. The threshold may be enlarged accordingly. `exact hCount` unpacks the family premise `CountF`, so the draft supplies no independent mass formula or count estimate.
