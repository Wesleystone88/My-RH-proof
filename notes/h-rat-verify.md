## What tensoring by one means here

**READ.** `Rows.Data` has a field `LL` and a ring homomorphism `embLL : LL →+* ℂ`. The types of `jEE` and `jN u` are semilinear over exactly this homomorphism. For each map the record requires that the images of its given rational basis be complex-linearly independent and span the complex target. [DataRows.lean:36](https://github.com/homeowmorphism/riemann-hypothesis-original/blob/4e8bb36faa8c9e3f1bda144ccf5a637f8b96066e/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataRows.lean#L36) · [RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean:185](https://github.com/homeowmorphism/riemann-hypothesis-original/blob/4e8bb36faa8c9e3f1bda144ccf5a637f8b96066e/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean#L185) · [RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean:189](https://github.com/homeowmorphism/riemann-hypothesis-original/blob/4e8bb36faa8c9e3f1bda144ccf5a637f8b96066e/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean#L189)

**DERIVED.** A unital map from a field to ℂ is injective. For either basis $(e_i)$, semilinearity gives $j(∑a_i e_i)=∑σ(a_i)j(e_i)$, where $σ=\mathrm{embLL}$. Consequently the unique complex-linear map from the scalar extension sending $e_i⊗1$ to $j(e_i)$ is an isomorphism. Under this identification j acts as $φ↦φ⊗1$. The Lean fields themselves are semilinear maps into concrete spaces. H-rat uses the chosen embedding, which plays the role of $σ_v$; identifying it with a separately specified manuscript place is part of the comparison of models. The manuscript’s named H-dict has its own specific automorphic-space and translation clauses.
