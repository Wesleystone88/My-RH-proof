E. Bookkeeping: n! supplies n/(p-1) = (p+1) * n/(p^2-1) factors of p.
   sl spends 1 share on denominators and keeps p shares   -> np/(p^2-1)  (full rate)
   sigma^2 wp spends p shares and keeps 1                  -> n/(p^2-1)   (slow rate)
   p shares spent = one term L_{p+1}u^{p+1} with a single 1/p, compounded by exp:
   (L_{p+1}u^{p+1})^m/m! costs m + m/(p-1) = mp/(p-1) factors of p at degree m(p+1), i.e. p/(p^2-1) per degree.
