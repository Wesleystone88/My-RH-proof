# Audit of the amplifier

**Verdict — CONDITIONAL.** The pointwise geometric ledger, character comparison, density bounds and host hypotheses imply \(E_{\mathcal L}(x)\le x^{1/2+9\varepsilon}\) on the stated windows. Equation (8) is the inequality to test independently. The first missing implication in a derivation from lift count and degree alone is the passage from a geometric degree inequality to a pointwise bound on the arithmetic cost of *every* base. That passage needs ten further ledger predicates, a compatible grid, and the error windows. One subsequent premise already contains a square-root estimate on a specified auxiliary set of characters. ## Which lift counts actually occur?

**READ / DERIVED [S5, S9, S13–S15].** The scalar theorem quantifies over *every* \(m\) and data satisfying its joint hypotheses. Its numerical interval contains \(\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor\) eventually. The rank-existence theorem quantifies differently. For each fixed \(0<\varepsilon<1/52\), each eligible scale and each frame of order \(x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le n\le2x^{2+8\varepsilon}\), it constructs ranks on that frame. Set \(A=\sqrt{x\,x^{-\varepsilon/8}}=x^{1/2-\varepsilon/16}\). The proof takes \(m=2\lceil A/2\rceil\), with \(A\le m<A+2\); RankVal itself requires \(A\le m\le2A\), and \(x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}\le k\le2x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}\). HostExW requires RankVal on its selected scales.

For fixed positive \(\varepsilon\), \(m/\sqrt x\le2x^{-\varepsilon/16}\to0\). Thus \(m\asymp\sqrt x\) in the usual constant-factor sense and \(m=\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor\) are both excluded eventually on this construction. “Comparable” in the weaker sense \(x^{1/2+O(\varepsilon)}\) does hold. Substituting the RankVal interval into (9) gives \(B/Q\le2x^{1/2+\varepsilon/16}+2+8x^{1/2+17\varepsilon/16}+2x^{1/2+9\varepsilon/8}\). All four terms satisfy the required scale before the explicit losses \(x^{C_*\varepsilon}/\delta\). Even a scalar choice \(m=\sqrt x\) would leave powers of \(x^\varepsilon\) in (9).

**READ [S1, S4–S6, S14–S15].** One \(C_D,x_0\) works for all indices at all sufficiently large \(x\) in a fixed LinkDIV family. The threshold in ch_core precedes the quantifiers over \(H,m,D\), after fixing \(C_*,\varepsilon,C_E,K_o,c,M\). The one in core_gm precedes the frame and host after fixing the level and budgets. Closed means \(\exists\varepsilon_1>0\ \forall 0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_1\ \exists K_{\rm family}\ \forall Fm,C_H\) satisfying class membership and \(Fm.{\rm Large}(K_{\rm family},C_H)\). The family bound here is a record of thresholds. HostExW provides \(\exists R,H,q_0\) jointly on a window; it gives no operation varying \(m\) on a fixed host. A change in \(m\) changes the lift grid, the rank \(r=k+m-1\), and the inputs to the ambient construction. One explicit higher dependence is \(\log{\rm GamP}(v)=C_\Gamma m^5 fL\). This is an internal input, distinct from an additional outer term in (8). Number field, prime choices, conductors, fibers and character representatives must satisfy the new record's conditions. The source neither forces each to differ nor guarantees they can all be retained. The target primitive character can be fixed before the host is chosen. **OPEN** is uniform control after an arbitrary re-selection outside these quantifiers, or in a joint limit \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon(x)\).

## The exact geometric prerequisite

**READ [S1–S4].** At a single record, LinkDIV_at is the conjunction below. Write \(S=\sum_t a_t,\ \lambda_w={\rm lwP}(w),\ e_w={\rm ramIdx}(w)\). All references to lifts range over \(1,\ldots,m\).

1. For every word \(v\), \(B_v(\nu_v^{\rm int})\subseteq cc\), the integral dual target.
2. \(s_{\rm frame}(\nu,h)+\mu_{\max}(RR)\le x^{C_H}b\), where \(s_{\rm frame}\) is the rank-weighted mean of \(\mu(\nu_v)-h(v)\).
3. For every \(v,j,w\) of residue characteristic \(p>X''\), with \(p\notin\{T(v),g_0,g_1,d\}\) and \(p\nmid D_\Gamma(v)\), both \({\rm gain}_{jw}\) and \(E_{vjw}\) vanish.
4. The complex pairing \(B_u^{\mathbb C}\) agrees with the embedded rational \(B_u\) on the rational inputs. There is one complex linear isometry \(V\) of the target test space and, for each \(u\), a nonzero scalar \(\alpha_u\) and a complex linear isometry \(U_u\) of its source test space such that \(B_u^{\mathbb C}(\phi,F)=\alpha_u\,{\rm besS}_u(U_u\phi,VF)\) for every \(\phi,F\).
5. The integral \(RR\) lattice is finitely generated and spans the target over its coefficient field.
6. For every \(v\) with \(B_v\ne0\), the mean over infinite places of the log squared operator norm of \(B_v:\nu_v\to RR^\vee\), in the specified metrics, is at most \(h(v)\).
7. The degree estimate is precisely (1).
8. For every \(v,j\), \(\big|\sum_{w\in{\rm supp}}2\lambda_we_w E_{vjw}-S\log(g_0g_1)\big|\le C_D B_0\).
9. For every \(v,v'\), \(\big|h(v)+S\log T(v)-h(v')-S\log T(v')\big|\le C_D(n_Ta L_a+m B_0)\).

## Two scalar tests

**COMPILED [S13].** Lean 4.33.1 kernel-checked the pinned declaration `proposition_size_bound` in isolation using the original `SizeWindows`, `GrowthSizeBound`, six scalar helper lemmas and original proof. Its reported axioms were `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Two independent real-algebra lemmas also passed: the four-term normalization in (9) and the absorption to the coefficient 26. The geometric package, character estimates and full repository headline theorem retain their separate evidence status.

**DERIVED [S5, S9, S13].** Exact rational-exponent arithmetic with \(x=2^{8192},\varepsilon=1/64,\ a_E=xb\) gives the following reproducible case. The base-two logarithms of \((m,n,k,n_T,n_2)\) are \((4088,17408,38144,17391,17280)\). These satisfy RankVal's three size intervals and the scalar size windows. The four logarithms for the terms of \(B/Q\) in (9) are \(4104,-25864,4234,3473\). With \(C_*=5\), \((2/\delta)x^{5\varepsilon}B/Q<2^{5005}\), while \(x^{1/2+9\varepsilon}=2^{5248}\). The exact square root is \(256m\). This tests the normalization without constructing the enormous \(b\) or an arithmetic host.

**DERIVED [S13].** There is a scalar countermodel to deducing \(mE\le x+m^2+m\sqrt x\) from (8) and its size restrictions. Take arbitrarily large even square \(x\), \(m=\sqrt x\), \(n=n_T=n_2=x^2,\ k=x^2,\ a_E=xb,\ \Sigma=0,\ E=4x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\), with fixed \(0<\varepsilon<1/52\). Then (8) has zero left side and positive right side. The proposed inequality would require \(4x^{1+\varepsilon}\le3x\). The test concerns scalar implication only. A stronger arithmetic result would have to remove the \(4x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\) comparison loss, control the omitted sum, and improve the density and error budgets enough to obtain the proposed coefficients. None of those improvements follows from optimizing \(m\).
